# Week 6 Student Reflection

Complete this after your final project demonstration. Short, specific answers are better than generic praise.

1. Which reading helped you most with the implementation? Name a concrete function where you applied it.
2. How does `get_legal_actions()` reduce the chance of an illegal computer move?
3. Give one example where a win and a defensive response might compete. Which rule takes priority and why?
4. Explain what your threat helper does and why it works on a copied state.
5. Describe the exact heuristic you used. Which parts are estimates rather than guarantees?
6. What test failed during development? What was the actual cause and how did you correct it?
7. What does your finished computer player **not** do yet? How might future search help?
8. What was already improved in the prepared starter, and what new code did **you** write?
