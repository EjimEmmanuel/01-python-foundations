# Week 6 Reading Map

Read these **before you work on the Stone Lines extension**. Make your own notes and answer the chapter exit questions. If you finish early, practice the non-game examples; do not start the project before the knowledge check.

| Order | Chapter | What to be able to explain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01_DECISIONS.md` | Rules, priorities, strategies and fallbacks |
| 2 | `02_STATE_AND_ACTIONS.md` | State, legal choices, action tuples, selection versus execution |
| 3 | `03_RANDOM_BASELINES.md` | Random choice, empty lists, baseline comparison |
| 4 | `04_RULES_PRIORITY.md` | Immediate wins, blocking threats, deterministic tie-breaks |
| 5 | `05_HEURISTICS.md` | Numerical action scoring and its limits |
| 6 | `06_AGENTS.md` | Environment, observation, policy, action cycle |
| 7 | `07_RULES_VS_ML.md` | Rules versus learning from examples |

**Suggested rhythm:** Chapter 1–2, then 3–4, then 5–7, with short demonstrations and discussion between readings. Move at the pace needed for understanding.

After all seven chapters, complete `../02_PRACTICE/KNOWLEDGE_CHECK.md` without looking at the reference solution. Ask the teacher to review your reasoning. Only then open `../03_PROJECT/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`.

### How to study a chapter
1. Read the goals and explain any unfamiliar words.
2. Predict the output of each example before executing it.
3. Write out a short explanation in your own words.
4. Complete the chapter's checks on paper/in your notebook.
5. Explain one idea aloud using an example outside Stone Lines.

### What not to do yet
Do not implement a minimax algorithm, search tree, new GUI, machine-learning model or database. Those are outside Week 6.
